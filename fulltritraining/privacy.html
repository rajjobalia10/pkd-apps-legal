Privacy policy

Summary

Ironman Trainer keeps everything on this iPhone. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared.

Information in the app

Your name (if you give one), your height, weight and the figure you picked, your race date, your answers from setup (experience, training days, focus sport, long ride day and units), the sessions you finish, skip or move, what you log for them (time, effort, distance, heart rate, notes and GPS routes), your workout and device settings, your race goal, your test history, workouts you add yourself, your test results and zones, your race-day plan, your race kit list, the guides you save, your reminder time and your settings are stored in a file inside the app on this iPhone. They never leave it.

Location and routes

If you track a ride or run with this iPhone, Ironman Trainer uses your location only while that workout is running, including with the screen locked (iOS shows a blue location indicator), to measure distance and pace and draw your route. The route is saved with the workout on this iPhone and never uploaded. The map is Apple Maps: to show it, the iPhone loads map images from Apple, under Apple's privacy policy, without your name or account. Turn location off any time in the iPhone's Settings.

Heart rate strap

If you pair a Bluetooth heart-rate strap, Ironman Trainer reads your heart rate from it during a workout and saves the average with that workout on this iPhone. iOS asks for Bluetooth permission first.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Ironman Trainer reads your workouts (type, time, distance and heart rate), for example a swim recorded on a watch, to fill in a session when you import it. It never writes to Apple Health, and Health data is never used for advertising, never shared and never leaves this iPhone.

Audio coach

Spoken cues use the voices built into iOS, on this iPhone. Nothing you say or hear is recorded.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, Ironman Trainer asks iOS for permission to show notifications and schedules them on this iPhone. They name that day's sessions. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else to make them. Turn them off any time in Profile → Reminders or in the iPhone's Settings.

What we don't collect

No contacts, camera, microphone, advertising identifier, device fingerprint, crash analytics or usage analytics. Ironman Trainer makes no network requests of its own, contains no third-party code or SDKs, and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Storage and backups

Device backups (iCloud or computer) may include the app's data, depending on your iOS settings; you manage those backups. Deleting the app, or Profile → Erase all data, removes its data from this iPhone.

Children

Ironman Trainer is a training app for adults. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Support

If you email support, Rivesa Solutions Private Limited receives your email address and anything you include, and uses it only to reply. Please don't include health or other sensitive information.

Contact and changes

Rivesa Solutions Private Limited, raj@rivesa.ai. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change; the date below changes when it does.
